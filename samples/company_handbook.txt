ACME ROBOTICS EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (SAMPLE)

Working Hours
Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Friday. Employees may choose their start time between 08:00 and 10:00. The office is closed on weekends.

Leave Policy
Every employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per year. Sick leave is 12 days per year and does not need a doctor's note for absences of two days or fewer. Unused annual leave up to 5 days can be carried over to the next year.

Remote Work
Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week with manager approval. The company provides a one-time home office allowance of 15,000 rupees.

Expense Reimbursement
Expenses must be submitted within 30 days. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 1,200 rupees per day. Flights must be booked in economy class.

Learning Budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 40,000 rupees for courses, books and conferences. Certification exam fees are covered if the exam is passed on the first attempt.
